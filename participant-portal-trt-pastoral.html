About this dataHow to read it

Each mob is one set of OptiWeigh scales on your farm. Growth rate is each animal's weight trend over the last 14 days, averaged across the mob. SeaFeed and Control are only compared over days when both mobs have a reading.

CO2e abatement is modelled, not measured directly. It estimates the methane avoided from each mob's lick block intake, using the Sea Forest product specification and a baseline emissions estimate from published research, then converts that to carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e) using the standard 100-year warming factor for methane (28). It is an estimate and will be refined as the trial continues. Total CO2e abated is the methane avoided per head to date, times the number of SeaFeed head, times that factor.

New mobs are tagged Early data for their first two weeks because small samples are noisy. A dash means a measurement doesn't exist yet. Lick block use, for example, is only worked out once a block has been visited twice.

Blocks in stock are the blocks delivered to your farm minus those put out. Blocks still on order are shown separately.
